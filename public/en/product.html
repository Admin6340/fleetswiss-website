The platform

More control. Less effort.

FleetSwiss is not a simple vehicle database but an operational platform for the daily work of fleet managers – from procurement through day-to-day operations to return.

Instead of spreading information across Excel lists, emails and separate systems, vehicles, drivers, contracts, costs and processes come together in one place.

Service models

Two ways to use FleetSwiss

FleetSwiss Full Fleet Software

You work with the platform yourself. The features are available to you according to the booked scope.

Managed Fleet

Complementary fleet management services tailored to your fleet – from support with procurement and contract processes to damage, return and partner processes. Scope and responsibilities are agreed individually.

  • Vehicle procurement: Support with vehicle orders and the handover of new vehicles.
  • Contract and document management: Support with leasing and contract processes and with filing vehicle documents.
  • Damage management: Handling of damage cases from report to closure.
  • Vehicle returns: Handling of returns including preparation and protocol.
  • Partners and garages: Support with coordination with partner garages and service providers.
  • Operational fleet administration: Support with administrative fleet tasks such as deadlines and checks.

Data migration

Structured import instead of Excel chaos

FleetSwiss handles the structured initial integration of your existing data – without duplicate entry and without media breaks.

  • FleetSwiss MASTER Import
    Central import of your vehicle and fleet data at the start.
  • Leasing provider & IFRS imports
    Transfer of existing leasing and IFRS data into FleetSwiss.
Supported data sources for import/workflow – currently as a structured import, not as a real-time API.
